How discounts are applied

Two discounts can reduce the programme fee (the {{ settings.application_fee|naira }} application fee included in it is fixed). They do not stack — the better one applies.

{{ settings.early_bird_pct }}%

Early-Bird Discount

Automatic when the programme fee is paid before the early-bird deadline.

Excellence Award

WAEC/NECO results from {{ settings.excellence_min_waec_year }}–date with {{ settings.excellence_min_as }} A's or more. Request it in your application, then bring the original result to TSCE. Admissions verifies it, admits you and applies 50% off.

Awards are decided before the programme fee is paid, so you always pay the right amount first time.
