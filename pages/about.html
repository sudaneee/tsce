About TSCE

Empowering people. Building skills.

Trust Skills Center of Excellence is a technology-focused training institution in Zaria, Kaduna State, equipping students, professionals and aspiring innovators with practical skills for the digital economy.

Our story

A centre of excellence for practical digital skills

TSCE was established to close the gap between education and employability. Our programmes are short, intensive and hands-on — designed with industry needs in mind so graduates can contribute from day one.

Located on the Zaria – Sokoto Road, we serve learners from across Northern Nigeria with training in software engineering, cybersecurity, data & AI, networking, cloud, hardware engineering, CAD and digital marketing.
