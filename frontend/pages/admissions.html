Scholarships & discounts

How discounts are applied

Two discounts can reduce the programme fee (the ₦5,000 application fee included in it is fixed). They do not stack — the better one applies.

15%

Early-Bird Discount

Automatic when the programme fee is paid before the early-bird deadline.

50%

Excellence Award

WAEC/NECO results from 2020–date with 5 A's or more. Request it in your application, then bring the original result to TSCE. Admissions verifies it, admits you and applies 50% off.

Awards are decided before the programme fee is paid, so you always pay the right amount first time.

General requirements

  • Basic computer literacy
  • Valid means of identification
  • Passport photograph (uploaded after admission)
  • WAEC/NECO result (required only for the Excellence Award)
  • Laptop recommended for software, data and cloud programmes
